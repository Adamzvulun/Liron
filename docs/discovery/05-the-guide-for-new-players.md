# 05 — A guide for new players

- **Date:** 2026-11-22
- **Adam's ask:** "We need to add some kind of guide that'll help a new player get comfortable and know his way around such a detailed and vast world of options."

## What a newcomer meets today

- **The first page** (the-road.md §8): a story card with one button to the Signal Tower's first lesson, a quieter one to the Caravanserai, and "Look around the square first".
- **The compass:** one line under the top bar saying what to do next, with a "2" for the other guild.
- **Three city errands:** a chest is waiting, gold for the Market, a wardrobe in Your House. Each appears once, when it becomes true.
- **Everything else, all at once:**
  - five places and the Arena on the square;
  - six numbers in the top bar;
  - a dock of five buttons on a phone;
  - two halls with a dozen stations each;
  - quests, contracts, chests, ranks, achievements, the Fellowship, challenges, events.

None of this says what anything *is*. Since invite links (ADR 0052), the newcomer is a friend on a phone who may never have played a game.

## What we learned

See [new-player-guides.md](../research/new-player-guides.md). In short:
- Up-front tours don't help.
- Tips at the moment of need, a character with a reason to be there, and introducing one thing at a time do help.
- A mark on everything is a mark on nothing, which we found ourselves on 2026-11-16.

## Open questions (answered by Adam, 2026-11-22)

| Question | Answer |
|---|---|
| Tour, Guidebook, or a guide character with tips? | A guide character with tips, plus a Guidebook |
| Lock places until they're reached? | No: all open, introduced in order |
| Who is it for first? | Invited friends |
| Who is the guide? | A new city character, not from either guild |
