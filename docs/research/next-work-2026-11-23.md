## Findings

### 1. Morse stops at 20 wpm, though ADR 0009 promises more

- **What the ADR says.** [ADR 0009](../decisions/0009-morse-speed-policy.md) says the character speed is "fixed at 20 wpm to start (user can raise it to 25+)", with a manual override in settings for every speed.
- **What the code does.** It has neither. `CHAR_WPM = 20` is a constant (`src/subjects/morse/lesson/progress.ts:12`), and every lesson, drill and trial plays at it. The only speeds above 20 are the callsign rush (up to 50 wpm, a burst game) and the Far Station and the finale (fixed speeds).
- **Why it matters.** Adam's goal is "send and receive without thinking: instant recognition, head copy, fluent sending". CW Academy's courses go on past this point:
  - the Intermediate course aims at "20 WPM or higher", with practice files up to 25 wpm and QSOs up to 30;
  - the Advanced course aims at 30 wpm and above, with head copy of longer exchanges and "copying behind" (writing one step after the sound) ([Intermediate curriculum](https://cwops.org/wp-content/uploads/2024/08/Intermediate-curriculum.pdf), [Advanced curriculum v2.2](https://cwops.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/08/CW-Academy-Advanced-Curriculum-v2.2.pdf), [Intermediate advisor advice](https://cwops.org/wp-content/uploads/2023/08/Intermediate-Advisor-Advice.pdf)).
- **The gap.** Once a player's Farnsworth speed reaches 20, the road has nowhere faster to go. That's an ADR promise not kept, so it gets a phase: [morse-speed.md](../game/morse-speed.md).

### 2. Arabic listening stops at single words

- **The goal.** The first one in the vision: "understand ... the Arabic spoken around him".
- **What listening practice is today:**
  - hear a word and pick its meaning (the word path);
  - hear a pair of sounds (the ear training);
  - shadow a sentence while it plays, and judge yourself (the echo well).
- **What's missing.** Nothing asks you to *understand a sentence by ear*, with no text in front of you.
- **What the deck has (measured from the deck, 2026-11-23):**
  - 4,823 items;
  - 536 of them are sentences of four words or more, each with a native recording;
  - the word path uses 241 of them.
- **The research is mixed.** Dictation-style tasks help some learners. A University of Tsukuba thesis traces many listening breakdowns to *word segmentation*, not hearing where one word ends and the next begins. That is what a "which words did you hear" task trains. But the studies are small, and one found no significant gain from full dictation ([Tsukuba thesis](https://tsukuba.repo.nii.ac.jp/record/2007991/files/DA010616.pdf), [BUAP thesis](https://repositorioinstitucional.buap.mx/bitstreams/9fd0178d-20b8-4e95-9e94-637c4cfa4190/download)). So the plan keeps it light:
  - pick the meaning, then put the words you heard in order from a word bank;
  - no typing.
- **Spec:** [arabic-listening.md](../game/arabic-listening.md).

### 3. Nobody hears when the game breaks

- **What's there.** `src/` has no error reporting: no `window.onerror` or `unhandledrejection` handler sends anything anywhere.
- **What that means.** Today a crash on a friend's phone is seen only if they tell Adam. With friends coming (#332), that is the weakest point.
- **Two ways to fix it:**
  - **Our own small log** in Supabase. It's free, nothing leaves our own database, and it can be capped.
  - **Sentry's free Developer plan.** About 5,000 errors a month and 30 days of history, for one user ([pricing review, read 2026-11-18](https://justinmckelvey.com/blog/sentry-pricing)). Not confirmed on Sentry's own page. It sends error data (pages, browsers, stack traces) to a third party, so under CLAUDE.md it's Adam's question.
- **Recommendation:** our own log, [ADR 0058](../decisions/0058-our-own-error-log.md) (Proposed).

### 4. A friend can't take their data or leave

- **What the database already does.** Every table's `user_id` references `auth.users` `on delete cascade` (for example `chat_lines`, `speech_checks`, `challenges`). So deleting a user removes their rows.
- **What's missing.** A friend can't do it themselves, and can't download what Waymark holds about them. For friends on someone's private site, that is basic courtesy, and it keeps Adam out of SQL.
- **Plan:** task 10.2.

### 5. Dependencies have drifted a little

- **Security.** `npm audit --omit=dev` finds 0 vulnerabilities.
- **Minor bumps.** Fourteen packages have them. Examples: Vite 8.3.4 → 8.3.8, Vitest 5.0 → 5.1, Playwright 1.63 → 1.65, supabase-js 2.118 → 2.119.
- **Majors to leave alone:** TypeScript 7 and `@types/node` 26. A major compiler change is not worth the risk without a reason.
- **Plan:** one refresh PR (task 10.3).

## Pre-mortem (`the-fool`): how this plan fails

| # | How it fails | Early sign | What the plan does about it |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Speed work built before Adam needs it.** He may not have reached 20 wpm with standard spacing yet, so 25 wpm sits unused. | His Farnsworth speed in the ledger is well under 20. | The faster speeds open only after 90% at 20 wpm with standard spacing, the ADR's own gate. Until then the setting only offers what ADR 0009 already promises (character speed 18–20, the override). Ordered last of the three phases. |
| 2 | **Listening drills feel like a test, not a game.** | Players open the station once and never again. | It's a station in the echo well with its own daily contract and events, like every other. Rounds are short (8 sentences). Replay and slower replay are free. |
| 3 | **The listening station leaks deck text** (ADR 0011). | Arabic of deck sentences appears in `content/` or commits. | It uses deck ids only, as the word path does. Friends without the deck see why the station is closed, as with the other deck stations (ADR 0050). |
| 4 | **The error log becomes a privacy problem or fills the database.** | Rows contain what players typed. The table grows past a few MB. | It stores only the message, the stack's first lines, the page, the app version and the browser. Never input text. Inserts go through an RPC that caps each player per day. The table keeps the last 2,000 rows. ADR 0058. |
| 5 | **Self-service delete is clicked by mistake**, or breaks a friend's view. | A friend's shelf errors after someone leaves. | It needs the password and a typed confirmation, and is tested end to end. Friends' views already handle a missing friend (a deleted user's rows cascade). |
| 6 | **Three phases at once dilute the work.** | Half-built PRs. | One phase at a time, in order, each with its own release. Fewer, larger PRs (ADR 0057). |

## The order recommended

1. **Phase 10, safe for friends (v1.8.0).** It's small, and it comes before friends arrive (#332): the error log, leaving with your data, the dependency refresh and the backlog tidy.
2. **Phase 11, hearing the street (v1.9.0).** Sentence listening, for the first goal in the vision.
3. **Phase 12, faster than thought (v1.10.0).** Morse past 20 wpm, the ADR 0009 promise.

The reasons: Phase 10 protects what is already built, before more people use it. Then the learning gaps, Arabic first because it's the first goal in the vision.
