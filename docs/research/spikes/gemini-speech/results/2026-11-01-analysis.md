## 1. Transcription (flash-lite)

Raw scores: sentence CER 9%, WER 25%; single-word CER 16%. WER overstates the errors, because the deck spells dialect in its own way. By hand:

**Sentences (30)**
| Verdict | Count | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| Exact (after normalising) | 12 | أنا الولد اللي بالنص · مكتب البريد بين البنك والسوبر ماركت |
| Spelling only | 7 | برّا→بره · عَ→على · بعملو→بعملوا · تلاتة→٣ (digits) |
| Meaning intact, small slips (often drifts towards MSA) | 5 | تلات→ثلاث · هادي→هذي · بناخد→ناخذ · آداب→أدب |
| Real word errors | 6 | فالت→فاتت · لو كانو مصاريّا بيكفّوا→لو كان مصاري بكفه · كتبت→كاتبت |
| …of which the meaning is lost | 3 | مش جاي عَ قدّي→مش جاية عبالي · أصحى بدري→اصحاب دري · في سياج→في شيء جاي |

→ **24/30 (80%) usable**, and 27/30 keep the meaning.

**Single words (20)**: 12 exact. 3 are spelling only: خمستاعش→خمسطعش, and قرش→غرش / قراية→جرية, where this speaker says ق as /g/ (*girš*, *grāya*) and Gemini wrote /g/ as غ or ج. 5 are wrong: بطفولتو→طوفان · ورقة→ورق عنب · الصومال→إش صوّال · استماع→اجتماع · جهل→جاهل.
→ **15/20 (75%)**. Isolated words with no context are clearly harder than sentences.

## 2. Pronunciation judgment (minimal pairs)

| Test | Result | What it means |
|---|---|---|
| Yes/no: "does the audio match this text?" (run.mjs) | accepts the correct text 100%, rejects the one-letter-wrong text **2%** | Useless: it says "yes" to almost anything. In one case its own reason named the error (غ vs ر) and it still answered "matches". In an earlier run with misaligned audio (see bugs below) it also "accepted" whole sentences that weren't in the clip. |
| Forced choice: "which of A/B was said?" with audio (choice.mjs) | flash-lite 46/50 (92%); 3-flash-preview 19/19 | Looks good, but… |
| Same forced choice with **no audio** (control) | flash-lite **48/50 (96%)** | …it does just as well without listening. The correct option is a real word and the swapped one usually isn't, so language knowledge alone solves it. |

**Conclusion:** native clips can't show whether Gemini hears a learner's mispronunciation, because there is no mispronunciation in them. Nothing here shows that it can. That needs recordings with real mistakes (phase 2).

## Bugs found and fixed while running
1. `.anki21b` packs **each media file** with zstd, so the "mp3s" were compressed bytes and Gemini got noise. Fixed in `prep.py`.
2. The media map holds a font file (`_BalooBhaijaan2-Regular.ttf`), so a regex over mp3 names shifted every later file by one. Six clips had another card's audio. Fixed by parsing the protobuf; the six were re-run.
3. The model auto-pick sorted names alphabetically and chose `gemini-omni-1.1-flash`, which has no free quota. It now picks the newest `gemini-X.Y-flash`. Also added a 90 s timeout, resumable checkpoints, and logging of rate-limit reasons.

## 3. Follow-up without a human voice (v2.mjs)
Adam chose free tier only and not to send his voice, so the follow-up uses only deck clips and synthetic audio.

**Dialect-aware prompt (v2)** on the same 50 clips ([table](2026-11-01-gemini-3.5-flash-lite-v2-deck.md)): sentence CER 9.1% → **7.5%**, WER 25% → 23%, single words flat (16% → 17%). It fixed dialect spellings (برّا, هادي) and some words (ورقة, استماع, طفولتو). But its "write what you hear, don't correct" rule also produced new non-words (معشي, الرعيّت, لقا). Use v1 (a normal listener) for "did it understand you?"; v2 only helps if you want raw sounds.

**Synthetic mistakes** ([json](2026-11-01-gemini-3.5-flash-lite-v2-synth.json)): free Gemini TTS said each sentence correctly and with one consonant swapped (ح→ه, ع→ا…), then flash-lite transcribed both with the v2 prompt. 15 pairs before the TTS daily caps ran out (~10/day per TTS model):

| Wrong-consonant audio came back as… | Count |
|---|---|
| the correct word (mistake fixed away) | **11** |
| unclear | 2 |
| the wrong consonant (caught) | 2 (both ط→ت, where the transcript is garbled anyway) |
| False alarms on the correct audio | 0 |

Caveat: the TTS may itself have "fixed" some swaps while speaking. Either way, the end result is the same: no sign that this chain can catch a swapped consonant.

**Final verdict: partial.** Use Gemini to check *understanding* of whole sentences, and give pronunciation feedback without AI (record-and-compare, ear training). See [ADR 0013](../../../../decisions/0013-speech-on-free-gemini.md).
