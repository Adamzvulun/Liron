## First-visit cards: places (§3.4)

- **The Notice Board:** Today’s quests and daily goal are posted here, with the contracts that pay gold. Chests you’ve earned wait on the shelf.
- **The Market:** Five wares are set out each day, the same for everyone. Gold buys gear for your traveler, furniture for your room, or a gift for a friend.
- **Your House:** This room is yours: tap the floor to walk. The cupboard holds your wardrobe, the shelf your trophies, the desk your ledger, and the letterbox the Fellowship.
- **The Arena:** Challenge a friend to beat your score, or take on one they’ve sent you. A challenge waits a week for its answer.

## First-visit cards and Guidebook chapter: The Signal Tower

Here you learn Morse code by ear, one letter at a time. Each lamp on the tower is a letter: light them all.

- **Lessons:** Letters play at full speed, with time between them; tap what you hear on the pad. Two runs in a row at 90% bring in the next letter.
- **Instant Recognition:** Name each letter before its lamp fades. The quicker you know a sound, the less you count dots and dashes.
- **Sending:** Key letters with the straight key: a short press is a dot, a long one a dash. The tower times your keying and shows your speed.
- **The Codebook:** Copy whole words as they’re sent, ten at a time. Your best score stays on the door.
- **The Radio Room:** Stations come on the air here every day. Answer one and hold a short contact, the way operators do.

## First-visit cards and Guidebook chapter: The Caravanserai

Spoken Palestinian Arabic is learned here, the letters first and then the words. Each corner of the courtyard teaches one thing.

- **The scribe’s table:** Each lesson brings a few new letters, their shapes and their sounds. Pass a lesson a few times in a row to move on.
- **The slate:** Write the letters you’ve learned. Writing them yourself helps them stick.
- **The innkeeper’s table:** Everyday words, a chapter at a time. Then the regulars who use them sit down to talk.
- **The fountain:** Hear a sound and pick the letter that makes it. Your ears learn the letters as your eyes do.
- **The echo well:** Two sounds that are easy to mix up, one after the other. Listen until you can tell them apart.
- **The storyteller:** Read short words made of the letters you know.
- **Coffee corner:** Everyday phrases, a few at a time, for greeting people and getting by.

## The Guidebook (§3.5)

Buttons: Show me around again · Take me there

### Finding your way

The line at the top of the square always says the one thing to do next. Each guild has a road, a story walked one step at a time, and the line follows the guild you practiced last. Every building is open: go in whenever you like.

### The Notice Board

Today’s quests and your daily goal are posted here. Each day brings three contracts, small tasks in the guilds that pay gold; finish all three for a chest. Chests wait on the shelf until you open them.

### The Market

Five wares are set out each day, the same for everyone: gear for your traveler from 60 gold up, a dye, a streak shield, and something for your room: furniture, a wallpaper or a floor. You can also buy a ware as a gift for a friend.

### Your House

Your own room: tap the floor or use the arrow keys to walk, and tap a piece to use it. The cupboard dresses your traveler, the desk keeps your ledger, the shelf your trophies, and the letterbox the Fellowship. Decorate moves anything in the room, turns a chair, and sets out the furniture, wallpapers and floors you own; Done keeps it, Cancel puts it all back, and each new thing you set out makes the room cozier for good. A room holds up to 20 pieces on the floor and 6 on the wall, and the way from the door must stay open; for 15 gems, stairs open a roof above it, a second room for your furniture. Friends can visit, walk around your room and leave a note in your letterbox (a few words of Arabic or Morse, one a day), but only you can change it.

### The Fellowship

Friends you invite are here, with what they’ve been up to. Invite someone from Settings: they make their own account and are your friend at once. Friends stand in the square, and tapping one visits their house: their room as they arranged it.

### The Arena

Challenge a friend to beat your score, or take on one they’ve sent you. A challenge waits a week for its answer.

### XP and levels

Every lesson and drill earns XP, and XP raises your level. Reaching a level pays 5 gold times the level, every 5 XP earns a gold coin, and every 5th level brings a gem.

### Gold and gems

Gold buys gear, furniture and streak shields at the Market. Gems are rarer: one every 5th level, one in the daily chest on every 7th day of a streak, and some for achievements. Nothing costs gems yet.

### Your streak

The streak counts the days in a row you’ve met your daily goal. A streak shield saves it on a day you miss: it costs 120 gold at the Market, and you can hold 2. Days 7, 30 and 100 of a streak each bring a chest.

### The daily goal

Choose how many minutes to practice each day, from 5 to 60: tap the goal in the top bar to change it. Reaching it pays the daily chest, 20 gold, with a gem on every 7th day of a streak.

### Ranks and achievements

Each guild has ranks, earned as you walk its road and pass its trials. Achievements are milestones across Waymark: they pay gems, and some give a title to wear under your name.
