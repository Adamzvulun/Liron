Line, shape, light

Ink outlines
Everything is outlined in Ink: 4 px on buildings and panels, 3 px on UI, 2 px on small detail. Never pure black.
Chunky bevels
Raised things get a light inner top edge and a solid ink drop below (4–6 px). Press = move down.
Light means alive
Lamplight glows only on what you can use or have earned. Locked places are desaturated stone.
Corners
8 px inside panels · 12–14 px cards and buttons · pills fully round. No sharp UI corners.
Motion
Slow ambient life (lamps flicker, steam rises), quick bouncy feedback (≤200 ms). Respect reduced motion.
Morse rule
No dot-and-dash charts anywhere. Morse is shown as sound and light, never as a picture to read.
