# Waymark 1.0.0 — The Crossing

- **Status:** draft, written 2026-11-16 (PLAN task 1.10). It becomes the text of the release PR (task 1.13). The date goes in when Adam merges.
- **What this release is:** the first one with something to learn. The live site has held only the empty city (v0.1.0) until now; 1.0.0 carries everything built since.

## What a player gets

### The square
- A painted city square with five places to walk into, and your own character standing in it.
- **One line that always says what to do next,** in the square, in both halls and at the end of every lesson. A first page greets a new player and points to the first step.
- Your level, XP, gold, gems, your streak and today's goal across the top. Streak shields save a missed day.

### The Signal Tower: Morse code
- **The Silent Beacon,** a story in ten chapters and 74 steps, from your first two sounds to live radio contacts.
- Lessons for 41 characters by ear, at real speed from the first day, with the gaps closing as you improve.
- Instant Recognition: name the letter before the lamp goes out.
- Sending with your own hand, on the space bar or a tap pad, read back as you key.
- The Codebook: whole words, call signs and the operators' shorthand, by ear.
- Five chapter trials, and three for rank: the Lamp Signal, the Night Watch and the Far Station.
- The Radio Room: contacts with six operators through noise and fading. With the site's AI switched on, they tell you their news and answer yours.

### The Caravanserai: spoken Palestinian Arabic
- **The Long Road In,** a story in 114 chapters and 274 steps, from the letters to an evening's talk.
- The script from nothing: every letter and vowel mark, heard, read and written on the slate, with Hebrew as the bridge.
- The echo well for the sounds Hebrew doesn't have, and shadowing a speaker's voice.
- Nine sets of survival phrases at the coffee corner, with real recordings. "Say it" listens and tells you whether you'd be understood.
- 3,390 words in 113 chapters at the innkeeper's table, in sentences with audio, reviewed at the moment you're about to forget them. Each chapter has a regular who tells you about their life, a trial, and a grammar card.
- Sit and talk: a written chat in Arabic with any regular you've met, who remembers what you told them. Afterward the Coach shows how a Palestinian would have said your lines.

### Your House, the Market and the Notice Board
- Your House: build your character's look, dress it in what you've earned, and keep a pet at its side.
- The Market: four wares a day for gold. Gold comes only from learning.
- The Notice Board: today's contracts, your quests, and the chests you've won.
- Ranks in both guilds, each with its own gear, and achievements with titles to wear.

### The look
- Everything is painted in the browser: the square, both halls, and a scene behind every station.
- No screen stands on a plain blue background any more. The page is wood, stone, tile or cobbles, and sign-in stands before the city gate at dusk.

## Good to know
- **The live site starts you fresh,** at level 1. What you did on the dev site stays there (your choice, 2026-11-16).
- **The AI parts need the site's keys.** Settings → Services says "ready" or "not set up on this site" for Sit and talk, Say it and the radio operators. Without keys the game still works; those three stay quiet or scripted.
- **Sit and talk is in writing.** The regulars don't speak aloud yet (#342).
- **The Arabic words need the deck.** They come from the Lingualism deck, which only its buyer may use; they are not in the code.
- **Not in this release:** friends and the Fellowship (2.0), the ledger of what you've learned (1.1), sound effects and city events (1.2), the Arena. The order is in [VERSIONS.md](../VERSIONS.md).
