# Waymark 1.3.1 — Nour, out of the way

- **Status:** released 2026-11-22 ([#426](https://github.com/Adamzvulun/Liron/pull/426)), merged by Claude once its checks passed, as with v1.2.1–v1.2.3. It is the text of the release PR (`release/v1.3.1` → `main`).
- **What this release is:** a fix release (X.Y.Z, [VERSIONS](../VERSIONS.md#how-to-read-a-version-number)) for v1.3.0's guide. The fixes come from a review of its code and from a newcomer's walk on a small phone. Nothing new to learn or set up.

## What a player gets

- **Nour's card no longer hides what Nour is talking about.** On a small phone the card covered the Notice Board, New mark and all, while Nour introduced it. On a phone the card now sits just under the top bar, over the sky, so the whole square's ground stays in view. On a wide screen it moves to the other corner while Nour introduces Your House, whose sign it covered.
- **Visiting a friend's house** no longer counts as going into Your House, so Nour's line about the Fellowship isn't lost on the way.
- **Opening a place from a link** (a bookmark, a reload) now counts as going in, so the place stops wearing its New mark.
- **A screen reader hears ", new"** on the place Nour is introducing.
- **If a guide card can't load** (a dropped connection, say), it's left out and the place still works. Before, the whole place showed an error. The Guidebook gets the same protection.
- **"Show me around again"** in the Guidebook is no longer held back by the first page.
- **On a phone, the Guidebook's list** doesn't mark a page as current until you choose one.
- **The Signal Tower's runs:** when a run tops up its sounds, it takes away a repeat of an older letter before one of the letter you're learning.

## For Adam

- **No migrations**, nothing to set up.
- **After merging:** on your phone, an account that hasn't had the welcome (or Guidebook → Show me around again) shows Nour's card under the top bar.
