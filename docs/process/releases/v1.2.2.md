# Waymark 1.2.2 — The square holds still on a touch screen

- **Status:** released 2026-11-21, merged by Claude once its checks passed (Adam's standing word). It is the text of the release PR (`release/v1.2.2` → `main`).
- **What this release is:** a fix release (X.Y.Z, [VERSIONS](../VERSIONS.md#how-to-read-a-version-number)): v1.2.1 only got halfway.

## What a player gets

- **The square stays open on an iPhone.** After v1.2.1 the square showed for a second, then Safari said a problem occurred and reloaded the page, over and over.
- **On a phone or tablet the square holds still:** no drifting clouds, smoke or flickering lamps, and the places don't fade or lift when tapped. A lamp lit by a road step still shows, for its two seconds. With a mouse, nothing changes.

## What was wrong

Every moving piece of the square (16 of them: clouds, smoke, flames) is a layer of its own, and in Safari everything painted over a moving piece becomes a layer too. The square's 8 stage-sized paintings and its 5 stage-sized places each became a full-size layer, and on an iPhone that is more memory than Safari gives a page. v1.2.1 removed the hover layers, which bought a second; once the pieces started moving, the rest came back. A still square is drawn as one picture.

Claude can't run Safari in the cloud session; this is inferred from what crashed, when, and from the Signal Tower (far fewer moving pieces) working on the same phone. A phone test checks that nothing in the square asks for a layer and nothing moves.

## Later

Motion on a phone can come back in a way that costs no layers (for example only the pieces nothing is painted over). That needs a real iPhone to measure, so it waits for a session where Adam can test.
