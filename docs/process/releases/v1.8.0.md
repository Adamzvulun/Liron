## What a player gets

- **Settings has a danger area.** **Download my data** saves one file with your progress, reviews, room and notes. **Delete my account** removes all of it for good, after your password and the word DELETE.
- **Faster Morse.** A speed dial in the Signal Tower sets how fast characters are sent, from 20 up to the fastest you've earned. A ladder of runs takes you to 35 wpm, with a metronome to wear at the top and the title Ghost. **The Long Copy** has lines of 9 or 11 words to copy in your head. The Radio Room's operators send at your speed.
- **The milestone check.** Every few weeks the compass offers it. You answer six "can you…?" statements per guild, then do a short test that doesn't count toward your progress. Each check is a point on your ledger, and the first one brings a wooden chest.
- **Spell it at the slate.** Once you've met every letter, you hear a word and type it. It's checked on the letters only, and after a wrong try your wrong letters are marked. There's a new daily contract: "Spell 2 rounds at the slate".
- **The inn's keyboard.** A new player types in Hebrew letters at first. After 3 Spell it rounds at 80%, the inn opens on the Arabic keyboard. A choice you've already made is kept.
- **Fixes in our own Arabic:** 17 typos in sentences, tales and a grammar card, found by a new check that compares each line's Arabic with its Latin.
