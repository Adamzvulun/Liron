## What a player gets

- **A friend who is home walks with you.** In their house, they walk, sit and go up to the roof where they really are, instead of standing by the window. The line under the room says "Basma is home.", or "Basma is home, up on the roof."
- **Whoever comes by is in your room.** At home, a visiting friend walks in your room, and a line says "Basma is here." Tap them and they wave.
- **A friend who isn't home** stands by the window, as before.
- **Reading Arabic with no choices.** Once a word's letters were all taught four lessons ago, every other such word in the scribe's table and the storyteller says **Read it aloud**. Say it, tap **Hear it** for the speaker, then answer **I read it right** or **Not yet**. Rank trials still check every answer themselves.
- **Verbs shaped like your binyanim.** At the innkeeper's table, 124 verbs carry a blue badge: "Shaped like hitpaʿel, הִתְלַבֵּשׁ", or piʿel, nifʿal, hifʿil. Tap it to see how the Arabic shows the shape, and other verbs of that shape from your chapters so far.
- **The regulars' tales.** Pass a regular's trial and they tell you a short tale, made only of words you know: Salim's greetings at the gate, Umm Khalil's questions, Hassan's bargaining. Find them under **Tales** at the innkeeper's table. Every regular on the road has one, all 113.
- **A quick Enter in the Codebook** is no longer lost on a busy phone.

## For Adam

- **One migration,** applied by DB migrate on `main`: `20270123000000_visiting_live.sql`. The wire's check (`may_use_wire`) also takes the new `visit:` channels, so only the two friends can join one.
- **What friends see:** [FRIENDS.md](../FRIENDS.md) now says that, while you're in a house, a friend in that same house sees where you walk and that you're home. It is never stored.
- **Costs nothing:** it runs on the same free Realtime as the wire, at a few messages a second per pair.
- **Spot-check the tales:** they're our own Arabic, like our own sentences. Read a few under Tales and tell Claude anything that sounds wrong.
- **After merging:** with a friend on two devices, one at home and one visiting, check that you see each other walk. The tests use a stand-in for Realtime, so this is the real test.

## Under it

- A private channel per friendship. A guest opens the line to the host, and a host at home opens one per friend (at most 40).
- Only whose house, the room, the cell and the seat go over the line.
- Also fixed: a home test that failed about one run in four on a phone.
- A house's lines go quiet when nobody is there, so an open house uses no Realtime messages.
- Claude's branches and release branches no longer deploy to Vercel ([ADR 0057](../../decisions/0057-fewer-deploys.md)).
