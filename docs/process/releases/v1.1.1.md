# Waymark 1.1.1 — Fixes from the code reviews

- **Status:** released 2026-11-19. Adam asked Claude to merge it ("Go ahead and merge both"), right after v1.1.0. It is the text of the release PR (`release/v1.1.1` → `main`).
- **What this release is:** a fix release (X.Y.Z, [VERSIONS](../VERSIONS.md#how-to-read-a-version-number)). Every part of the code had a high review on 2026-11-19, and about 60 real bugs were fixed ([report](../reports/2026-11-09.md) §4df–4dt). Adam's own find, the sign-in screens, is in it too. Nothing new in kind.

## What a player gets

- **Sign-in works the way sign-in works everywhere.** "Forgot your password?" opens its own step with "Send reset email". Setting a password asks for it twice (New password, Confirm password) and names the account. Every password box has Show/Hide and warns when Caps Lock is on. The words are the standard ones: Sign in, Incorrect email or password, Passwords do not match.
- **A run that couldn't be saved no longer loses its XP** or its one-time rewards. The save is sent again and counted once.
- **Your name can't be wiped** by opening Settings before your profile has loaded, and **a purchase can't be undone** by a late answer from the board.
- **The finales' gear is earned, never bought** or found in a chest, and a finale counts only after the rank that opens it.
- **The Caravanserai keeps your good days** on the word path across a reload; earlier phrases come back for review; the slate's "from memory" step no longer shows the letter.
- **"Say it" works in Firefox.**
- **No sound, no dead end:** if the browser refuses sound, every Morse station lets you go on.
- **The road announces a step once,** not again on every "Play again".
- **Easier to read and reach:** faint hints are darker; the halls' paintings pan from a keyboard on an upright phone; the daily-goal chip and the compass's count say what they show to screen readers and voice control. Confirmations say Cancel; pop-ups close with Continue.
- And many smaller ones: double clicks in the Fellowship count once, the party feed keeps working past 100,000 lines, QRS reaches every operator, the operators' news keeps its numbers, the square's golden haze shows.
