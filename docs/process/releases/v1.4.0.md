## What a player gets

- **Your House is a room you walk in.** Tap the floor, or use the arrow keys, to walk. Your pet follows a step behind, and the room keeps you clear of the furniture.
- **The pieces do things.** The cupboard opens your wardrobe, the shelf your trophies, the desk your ledger and the letterbox the Fellowship. You can sit on the bed, a chair or the bench, and the lantern puts its light out and lights it again. The list "In this room" reaches any of them without walking.
- **Decorate.** Pick up any piece and set it down somewhere else: tap it, then a spot; or drag it; or use the arrow keys and Enter. Store the furniture you don't want out, and take it back from the tray. Done saves the room; Cancel puts it all back. The room says why a piece can't go somewhere ("That would block the way to the cupboard.").
- **Furniture, wallpapers and floors to collect.** Twelve pieces and six room styles join the Market's wares (one a day, as the fifth ware) and the chests. Each guild's first rank gives its banner to hang.
- **Visit a friend's room.** A friend's house is their real room, as they arranged it, with them standing by the window. Walk around it, sit down, light their lamp for yourself and look at their shelf. Nothing you do there changes their room.
- **A name to start with.** A new player gets a random Arabic name (Tawfiq, Dawood, Salma and the like) until they choose their own in Settings. Existing players keep their names.
- **Nour knows the home.** Nour points at Decorate once you own something for your room, and the Guidebook's "Your House" page covers walking, the pieces, decorating, the room's limits and visits.
