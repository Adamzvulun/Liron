# Waymark 1.6.0 — The roof

- **Status:** built on `dev` 2026-11-23. Claude merges the release once its checks and axe are green, at Adam's word to decide releases himself (2026-11-23).
- **What this release is:** the last part of Phase 8, epic [#468](https://github.com/Adamzvulun/Liron/issues/468): 8.4, a second room ([#472](https://github.com/Adamzvulun/Liron/issues/472)). Spec: [the-home.md](../../game/the-home.md) §16.

## What a player gets

- **A roof of your own.** Under your room, **The roof…** offers a flat roof over the city at dusk, with the Signal Tower beyond the low wall. It opens once, for 15 gems. Until you have enough, it says how many more you need.
- **A second room to decorate.** Take the stairs up and set out your furniture there. Pictures, banners and the clock hang on the neighbors' walls. Each piece stands in one room at a time, so the tray on the roof shows what isn't downstairs. The roof keeps its own lime walls and slabs.
- **Coziness across both rooms.** Anything new you set out on the roof makes your home cozier too.
- **Friends' roofs.** In a friend's house, **Up to their roof** shows the roof they opened.

## For Adam

- **One migration,** applied by DB migrate on `main`: `20270122000000_roof.sql`.
  - It adds a room to each saved home (every saved room becomes the house).
  - Opening the roof takes 15 gems.
  - Saving, reading and visiting take the room, and an older open tab still saves the house.
- **What friends see:** [FRIENDS.md](../FRIENDS.md) now says friends see your roof's arrangement once it's open, and your coziness counts both rooms.
- **Why 15 gems:** all 24 achievements pay 60 gems together, and chests give one now and then. Nothing spent gems before. 15 gems is a few weeks of play.
- **After merging:** if you have 15 gems, open your roof, take a chair up, and visit a friend's roof once they have one.

## Under it

- The roof is painted on the same grid and perspective as the house, so every piece stands and hangs there unchanged.
- The browser and the database check the roof's rules on the same test cases, as for the house.
