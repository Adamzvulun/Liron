# Waymark 1.5.0 — The home lived in

- **Status:** ready for release 2026-11-23. Claude merges it once its checks and axe are green, at Adam's word to decide releases himself ("These questions are the kind of questions I told you to deal with yourself", 2026-11-23).
- **What this release is:** the first part of Phase 8, epic [#468](https://github.com/Adamzvulun/Liron/issues/468), spec [the-home.md](../../game/the-home.md) §13–15. The second room, the roof (8.4, #472), comes in a later release.

## What a player gets

- **Notes at the letterbox.** In a friend's room, the letterbox now says "leave a note". You pick one of ten:
  - five short Arabic sentences, shown in script, its reading and its meaning;
  - three Morse sign-offs (73, CUL, TNX);
  - two plain lines.

  You can leave one a day per friend. Your own letterbox keeps the notes friends left you, and the Notice Board says "Basma stopped by your room".
- **Coziness.** Every new piece of furniture, wallpaper or floor you set out makes your room cozier, for good. Storing things never lowers it. It climbs from "Just moved in" to "Warm as fresh bread", and shows under your room, with how many more new things the next step needs. A friend's shelf shows how cozy their room is.
- **Turn a chair.** In Decorate, a chair now turns four ways and the window seat two, with the Turn button or R.

## For Adam

- **Three migrations,** applied by the DB migrate run on `main`:
  - `20270119000000_notes.sql`: the letterbox notes. Only the friend who wrote a note and the friend it is for can read it. Notes are deleted after 60 days.
  - `20270120000000_coziness.sql`: what each room has ever held, backfilled from the rooms saved today, and the count on a friend's card.
  - `20270121000000_facings.sql`: three new ways a piece can face. Saved rooms are unchanged.
- **What friends see:** [FRIENDS.md](../FRIENDS.md) now covers notes and coziness. A note is never free text, and a friend sees your coziness as a name and a number, never the list of what you own.
- **After merging:** visit a friend and leave a note, then look at your letterbox when they leave you one. Turn a chair in Decorate.

## Under it

- A note is a short code from a fixed set, like the feed's rows (ADR 0043). Nothing anyone types reaches a friend's screen.
- The database and the browser count coziness the same way, and a test holds them equal on every test layout.
- An old browser tab that hasn't reloaded since this release can't read a turned chair, so it shows that player the starter room until it reloads.
