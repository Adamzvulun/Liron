## What a player gets

- **Nour the lamplighter** stands by the compass on the square, in a blue robe with a lantern on a crook. Nour is a guide, not a teacher: they say what a place is for, then step aside.
- **A three-line welcome,** after the first step or after "Look around the square first": the compass line, the top bar, the buildings. Each line rings what it talks about. It can be skipped.
- **Introductions, one at a time:** the first place whose moment has come wears a small **New** mark (the Notice Board first, the Fellowship once a friend is in town, the Market once there's gold for a ware, and so on), and Nour says one line about it. Every place stays open the whole time.
- **First-visit cards:** the first time a player opens the Notice Board, the Market, Your House, the Arena, a hall or one of its stations, two sentences say what it's for. Not in the way: any action closes it.
- **The Guidebook:** thirteen pages, from Settings (Guidebook, Open) or by tapping Nour when there's nothing new. A page for each guild with its stations, each place, each number in the top bar, and ranks. Every number in it is read from the game, so a price change can't leave it wrong. **Show me around again** replays the welcome.
- **Players who were here before** aren't greeted as strangers: what they've clearly already found (gear owned, a changed look) counts as seen.
