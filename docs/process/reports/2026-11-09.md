- **#46, daily contracts:**
  - **What you'll see:** three contracts a day on the Notice Board. Each shows who gives it, the task, your progress ("1/3", or "Done") and the gold.
  - **In the HUD,** one line: "1 of 3 done today · 30 gold left to earn".
  - **Which three:** one habit (minutes, sessions, or two subjects), then one from each subject you've started, or two from it if you've started only one. Never one for a subject you haven't opened. They're fixed for the day.
  - **Gold only, never XP.** 10 to 20 gold each, about 45 for a full day. The full list is in [economy](../game/economy.md#daily-contracts).
  - **Paid by the database** as soon as a contract is done, once. The browser can't claim gold (ADR 0029).
  - **The chest for finishing all 3** comes with #48. Until then the board only promises the gold.
