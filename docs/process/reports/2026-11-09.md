  - Before that, the plain line shows as before.
  - On phones it's behind the dock's Quests button.
- **#46, daily contracts:**
  - **What you'll see:** three contracts a day on the Notice Board. Each shows who gives it, the task, your progress ("1/3", or "Done") and the gold.
  - **In the HUD,** one line: "1 of 3 done today · 30 gold left to earn".
  - **Which three:** one habit (minutes, sessions, or two subjects), then one from each subject you've started, or two from it if you've started only one. Never one for a subject you haven't opened. They're fixed for the day.
  - **Gold only, never XP.** 10 to 20 gold each, about 45 for a full day. The full list is in [economy](../game/economy.md#daily-contracts).
  - **Paid by the database** as soon as a contract is done, once. The browser can't claim gold (ADR 0029).
  - **The chest for finishing all 3** comes with #48. Until then the board only promises the gold.
- **#48, part 1: what you own, and streak shields** ([PR #139](https://github.com/Adamzvulun/Liron/pull/139)).
  - **The 4 locked gear items are now really earned.** Before, they only showed their condition and nothing gave them. Now the headset comes at level 5 in the Signal Tower, the cloak on a 7-day streak, the boots after 10 daily contracts (it said "10 quests" before), and the breeches when you pass a first chapter's trial. The database gives them, and the wardrobe lets you wear them.
  - **Streak shields:** the HUD shows how many you hold next to your streak, and the Quests panel says "A streak shield saved your 12-day streak" when one covered a missed day. You can't get shields yet: chests (next) and the Market will give them.
  - **Trials you've already passed count:** each save lists the trials passed so far, so the breeches arrive with your next Koch run or letters session.
- **#48, part 2: reward chests.**
  - **Where they come from:** finishing all 3 of a day's contracts gives a wooden chest. Each trial you pass, and reaching a 7-day streak, gives a brass one. 30 and 100 days give a jeweled one. Each source gives its chest once.
  - **On the Notice Board,** a Chests shelf lists what's waiting and why ("Signal Tower: chapter 1 trial passed"). Today's contract chest sits there locked, with a seal lit for each contract done.
  - **Opening one:** the lid lifts and what's inside rises one by one: gold, maybe a gem, maybe gear you don't own yet, maybe a streak shield. Collect closes it.
  - **In the HUD:** "Chests · 2 to open", linking to the board.
  - **The database rolls it when you open it,** the same every time for that chest, so reloading can't reroll it, and opening it twice pays once. Odds are in [economy](../game/economy.md#reward-chests).
  - This is where streak shields first come from.

Rename any you like.

- **#49, part 2: buying.**
  - **Walk into the Market** from the square: a striped stall with today's 4 wares, 2 gear, 1 dye and the streak shield. The same 4 for everyone, new each day.
  - **Each ware shows your own traveler wearing it.** Tap the price to buy; the gold leaves your purse and the item is in your wardrobe at once.
  - **What you can't afford yet** says how much more gold you need ("50 more gold"). What you own says "Yours"; the shield says "You hold 2" when you're full.
  - **One change from the concept:** the stall is in the Market building, not on the Notice Board. The board already holds contracts, questlines and chests. Say if you want it on the board too.
  - **The database checks everything** (today's stock, the price, what you own), so the browser can't fake a purchase.
