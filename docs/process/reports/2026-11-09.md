- **#137, speed: measured.**
  - **How:** a local build with a fake Supabase that answers 70 ms late, like Frankfurt from Israel. I tried two profiles: a desktop, and a phone (4× slower CPU, slow 4G). The dev site itself sits behind Vercel's login, so I couldn't trace it directly.
  - **Desktop, first open:** the square shows in about 0.5 s. Everything is loaded by 0.7 s, or 0.57 s with the fix below.
  - **Desktop, coming back to the square from any place:** about 0.2 s, every time. That's the browser redrawing the city's **5,871 vector shapes** (1.4 MB of drawing). It's the biggest cost I found.
  - **Phone, first open:**
    - The square shows at about 2.6–3 s.
    - About 1.2 s is downloading the 189 kB main script.
    - About 1 s is painting the city: 0.28 s to draw it in code, 0.7 s for the browser to lay it out.
    - Then the data arrives.
  - **Fixed now ([#144](https://github.com/Adamzvulun/Liron/pull/144), merged):** the wallet, profile, day and contracts used to load in 4 rounds, one after another. They now load at once, and everything arrives about 0.15 s sooner on desktop (more on a slow line).
  - **Tried and dropped:**
    - *Showing the city's scenery as images made in the browser:* the page got 4,000 elements lighter, but first open on a phone got slower (3.2 s), because decoding the images happens up front.
    - *Keeping the city in the page while you're in a place:* coming back took about the same time, since the redraw is what costs.
    - *Preloading fonts:* no gain on desktop, and slower on phones, where the fonts take bandwidth from the script.
- **#141, #142 and #143 merged.** Thanks for merging #142.
- **#137, hovering the buildings: faster.** You said the buildings were slow to react and light up on hover. I measured what each hover did:
  - **The cause:** on every hover the browser made the building a separate picture and drew it again before anything moved. That was about 0.6 s of drawing over 16 hovers, on a slowed machine. Then the gold outline faded in over 0.18 s, starting slowly.
  - **The fix:** each building is kept ready as its own picture, so lifting it just moves it. Drawing during hovers fell from about 0.6 s to about 0.13 s over the same 16 hovers. The outline now lights up in 0.08 s and fades out at the old pace.
  - **What's left:** the gold outline is blurred again each time it appears. It's small now.
  - **The test that guards the square's speed** now hovers like a person does: onto each building, a pause, then off. The old one swept the mouse across the square and missed this cost.
