**How the translations go wrong:**
- **The deck's English only makes sense in its section, and the app drops the section.** وزير shows "queen" (chess), بار "a barbell" (gym), زيتي "olive" (the colour).
- **For words spelled the same, the app keeps whichever comes first.** بابا shows "pope" (it's also dad), أمّي "illiterate" (it's also my mom).
- **Verb forms are glossed as infinitives.** بِدّو shows "to want", but it means "he wants".
- **Grammar tags leak into the romanisation.** You see things like "lúwaz [pl.]".
- **Early lessons include words that don't belong there,** such as snot, urine and AIDS.

**Nothing is fixed yet.** You asked for these to be documented first, and they're the first items for the next session.
