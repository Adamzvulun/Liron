## 2. Needs your approval (I picked what I thought you'd pick)
1. **The Arabic lesson design** (section 7 of `arabic-script-lessons.md`):
   - Letters are taught by shape family: ا ب ت ن ي first.
   - Read choices start in Hebrew letters (with niqqud), then fade to Latin.
   - There's no audio for letter names, only sounds.
   - A lesson passes at 90% twice in a row, the same as Morse.
2. **The courtyard's look:** it's the painted concept you saw, now live.
3. **The chapter I story** (`content/arabic/questline.json`). **One change from the draft:** the chapter now covers all 8 lessons, one milestone each.
4. **The Painted Sign trial:** 30 words at 90%, +50 XP, rank غريب Stranger → ضيف Guest.
5. **The fountain and the storyteller** are practice rounds of 12 that **don't count toward passing a lesson**. Only the scribe's table does.
6. **Vowel marks:**
   - When each mark is taught: fatha, kasra and damma in lesson 1, sukun in 2, shadda in 3, tanween in 5.
   - From lesson 4, every other word is shown bare. The deck writes almost no fatha (118 of 1,803 words), so you need to read bare words early.
7. **The slate's check is rough on purpose:**
   - A trace passes at 75% coverage.
   - Writing from memory passes at 55%.
   - It checks shape, not stroke order.
8. **The Latin fade:** choices turn Latin 2 lessons after a word's letters were taught.
   - The last step, with no choices at all (you type or say the word), waits for the speech decision below.
9. **The coffee corner:**
   - The 9 phrase sets (`content/arabic/phrases.json`, listed in `docs/game/survival-phrases.md`).
   - Speaking is **judged by you** ("Close enough" or "Again"). Your recording stays in the browser and is never uploaded.
   - A set passes at 90% understanding, +20 XP.
10. **The E10 draft and its first concept** (below, section 6). It's design only; nothing is built.
11. **Release v1.0.0?** Morse and the whole Arabic script course (letters, marks, writing, phrases) are playable on `dev`. I think that's "something to learn", but releasing to `main` is your call.
