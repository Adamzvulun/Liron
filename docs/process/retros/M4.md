# Retro: M4 The people

- **Dates:** 2026-11-10 (the Radio Room built) to 2026-11-15 (the last polish before the v1.0 plan).
- **Epics:** E15 the AI partner, E16 the Radio Room, E17 talking with the regulars. Alongside them: art for every screen (A1–A6) and the Arabic word path growing from 34 chapters to 113.
- **Written on 2026-11-16** for the v1.0.0 release (PLAN task 1.10), by Claude from [HISTORY.md](../HISTORY.md), the reports and the code. Adam's view is still to come; add it here.

## What we built
- **The Radio Room (E16):** real contacts with six operators, with noise and fading, built first with scripted lines only ([design](../../game/radio-qso.md)).
- **The AI partner (E15):** one server function that holds the keys and the prompts. The operators tell their news and answer yours (ADR 0036, 0037). For the operators it asks Gemini's free tier first, with Groq as the backup; there is a daily cap per player, and a scripted line whenever the AI fails.
- **Sit and talk (E17):** a chat in Palestinian Arabic with the regular of any chapter you have passed, who remembers what you told them; then the Coach, who shows how a Palestinian would have said your lines (ADR 0038). Chat lines go to Groq only. You can say a line aloud.
- **A painting behind every screen:** Your House, the Market, the Notice Board, the Radio Room, the innkeeper's table and the listening desk.
- **The word path to 3,390 words,** each chapter with its regular, its trial and grammar cards; the upper ranks in both guilds.

## What worked
- **Scripted first, AI second.** The Radio Room worked end to end before any model was called, so the AI could only add to it. Every AI line still has a scripted fallback.
- **Free tiers held.** Daily caps in the database keep each player inside the free limits; nothing has cost money.
- **One function, keys on the server.** The browser never sees a key or a prompt. The same pattern served the operators, the regulars and the Coach.
- **Design documents before code,** again: each epic had its page in `docs/game/` and an ADR.

## What didn't
- **We couldn't see whether a key was set.** The dev site sits behind Vercel's login, so Claude can't call it, and a missing key looked like "busy". Adam had no way to check either. v1.0 adds a line in Settings that says what is switched on (task 1.8).
- **The regulars have no voice.** They were meant to speak aloud. No free, good Palestinian voice was found; it is parked until Adam has looked at Azure (#342).
- **Blue everywhere.** The paintings went in, but cards still sat on a navy page in many places, and Adam asked for it gone. Fixed in v1.0 (task 1.5).
- **Breadth outran the thread.** M4 added three more things to do and no single "do this next". Adam's answer was the rule behind v1.0: the player must never feel lost.
- **Learning results: still none measured.**
