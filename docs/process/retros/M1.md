# Retro: M1 Foundations

- **Dates:** 2026-11-02 (Adam said go) to 2026-11-03 (PR #68 merged).
- **Epics:** E1 project setup (#2), E2 platform core (#3), E3 learning engine (#4), E4 city v0 (#5) and E5 avatar v0 (#6). All are closed.
- **PRs into `dev`:** #50, #51, #53–#68.

## What we built
- **Setup:** Vite, React and TypeScript, with tests (Vitest and Playwright) and CI, hosted on Vercel. It has two live sites: `main` and `dev` (ADR 0015).
- **Platform:**
  - invite-only login (Supabase)
  - the player schema with automatic migrations
  - the subject contract
  - XP, levels, gold, gems, the streak and the daily goal
- **Learning engine:**
  - FSRS spaced repetition
  - Glicko-2 skill ratings
  - the content pipeline: the whole Lingualism deck (4,823 items with audio) sits privately in `waymark-dev`
- **The city:** painted in the browser from the approved concept, with clickable places, the HUD and a phone layout.
- **The avatar:** a paper-doll character, and a wardrobe in Your House.

## What worked
- **Design first, then build.**
  - Adam reviewed the city (#27) and the avatar (#28, #29) as working concepts, screenshotted at desktop and iPhone sizes.
  - His review of the city caught real sizing problems: the scene didn't fill the window, the Arena had flags, and the phone HUD was too tall. They were fixed before merge.
- **Looking at every screenshot before showing it.** This caught bugs that would otherwise have reached Adam, for example the avatar thumbnails spilling out of their tiles and the Save button hidden behind the mirror.
- **Tests in CI on two screen sizes.** CI caught a phone layout bug (`min-height: 0`) that local runs missed.
- **Automate everything.**
  - Migrations apply themselves.
  - Content uploads run in CI, 8 audio files at a time (PR #64).
  - Adam's hand steps were limited to accounts, secrets and approvals.
- **ADRs for every choice** (0015–0027). They kept sessions consistent across handoffs.

## What didn't
- **A whole milestone with nothing to learn.**
  - M1 built foundations only: a user can walk the city, but no place teaches anything yet.
  - That was the plan, but it means there's no evidence yet that Waymark teaches.
  - **Learning results: none yet.** The first measurable learning comes with M2.
- **Claude suggested a release too early.** After M1, Claude proposed releasing `dev` to `main`. Adam's rule is to release only when there's something to learn. It's now written down in STATUS and in [ADR 0027](../../decisions/0027-release-versioning.md).
- **Environment friction:**
  - `git push` sometimes hangs (we now run it with `timeout 60` and retry).
  - Tag pushes are refused, so Adam creates tags.
  - npm 10 crashes resolving vitest's peers (we install with npm 12).
  - None of these blocked us, but each cost time the first time.
- **Stacked PRs (#63, #65–#67):** these needed fixes merged forward through the stack. It works, but it's fiddly. Keep stacks short.

## What we change for M2
1. **Ship something learnable first, polish second.**
   - E6 (hear Morse) and E8 (Arabic letters) come early, so v1.0.0 (ADR 0027) can follow as soon as Adam is happy with them.
   - Which slice of M2 makes the release is Adam's call.
2. **Measure learning from day one.**
   - Every lesson records what's reviewed and how well. FSRS and Glicko-2 already store this.
   - The M2 retro should answer "is it teaching Adam?" with numbers, not impressions.
3. **Keep design-first for every new screen.** Lesson screens get a screenshotted concept and Adam's approval before they're built out.
4. **Release only when there's something to learn,** numbered by ADR 0027.
