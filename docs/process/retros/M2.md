# Retro: M2 First playable

- **Dates:** 2026-11-03 (the Morse sprint began) to 2026-11-09 (E10's last part merged; The Silent Beacon finished in #148).
- **Epics:** E6 Signal Tower: receive (#7), E7 Signal Tower: send (#8), E8 Caravanserai: script (#9), E9 survival phrases (#10) and E10 quests and rewards (#11).
- **Written late**, on 2026-11-16 for the v1.0.0 release (PLAN task 1.10), by Claude from [HISTORY.md](../HISTORY.md), the reports and the code. Adam's view is still to come; add it here.

## What we built
- **Morse:**
  - a sound engine, and Koch lessons at 20 wpm with slower spacing that tightens as you improve (ADR 0009, ADR 0028);
  - Instant Recognition;
  - sending with a straight key (space bar or tap), read by a decoder that learns your speed;
  - the painted tower hall, and The Silent Beacon with a trial at the end of each chapter.
- **Arabic:**
  - the script taught in-house: every letter and the vowel marks, with Hebrew as the bridge (ADR 0012);
  - Meet, Listen and Read lessons, and the slate for tracing and writing from memory;
  - survival phrases at the coffee corner, with the deck's audio;
  - "Say it": a recording checked by Groq Whisper through our own server function (ADR 0014);
  - the painted courtyard, and The Long Road In, chapter I.
- **The game around them:** daily contracts on the Notice Board, reward chests, and the Market selling four wares a day for gold.

## What worked
- **One theme per sprint, never mixed** (Adam, 2026-11-03): Morse, then Arabic, then quests. Each sprint ended with something that could be played.
- **A concept first, screenshotted, before the build.** The lesson screen and the Notice Board were approved as pictures.
- **The full review on 2026-11-04.** Adam asked for specialist reviews of everything so far. It found real problems while they were cheap: rewards could be written by the browser (now only the database writes them), and a Morse run could be passed by mashing keys.
- **Content as data.** Letters, phrases and characters live in `content/`, checked by tests. The deck's text stays out of the code and the commits (ADR 0011).

## What didn't
- **Speed.** Adam said the site felt slow (#123, then #137). The square was drawn as thousands of shapes with blend modes on top. It took two rounds of measuring to fix, and it only got a written budget in v1.0 (task 1.7).
- **The stations multiplied faster than the story.** By the end of M2 each hall had several stations that opened by a rule and belonged to no chapter. A new player could not tell what to do next. That was fixed only in v1.0, by the Road and the compass.
- **"Adam tried it" stayed open.** The script lessons (#38, #39) were built and tested but not played through by Adam, so the milestone closed without his sign-off on them. They are on the v1.0 release checklist.
- **Learning results: none measured.** The first milestone that teaches closed without evidence that it does.

## Next time
- Write the path through a milestone's features (what a player does first, second, third) before building the features.
- Give speed a number at the start, not after a complaint.
- Close a milestone only after Adam has played it; put his playthrough in the plan as a task.
