## What we built
- **Morse (E12):**
  - word lists, prosigns in the engine and decoder;
  - the Codebook word drill and the callsign rush;
  - daily contracts for them.
- **Arabic (E11):**
  - a 1,020-word path in 34 chapters, with word lessons at the innkeeper's table;
  - grammar cards and the grammar book;
  - a regular with a trial for every chapter (The Long Road In II–XXXV);
  - 867 of our own example sentences for the words the deck has none for (text only).
- **Ranks (E13):**
  - the rank ladder in both halls, with rank gear;
  - achievements, gems and titles;
  - skill ratings beside the rank;
  - three Morse rank trials: the Lamp Signal, the Night Watch and the Far Station.
- **Mic practice (E14):**
  - the echo well (sound pairs);
  - shadowing;
  - "Say it yourself", with the shadow check;
  - contracts and achievements for all of it.

## What worked
- **Design doc first, then one issue per task.** Every epic started with a doc in `docs/game/`, so the overnight runs had a clear target and Adam could review the calls in one place.
- **Content as data, checked by tests.**
  - A test checks every path word, every regular and every own sentence.
  - The sentence checker flagged any word not in the deck or not yet met, and each flag was looked at by hand.
- **Fake database in e2e tests.** Whole flows (trials, ranks, contracts) were tested without a real backend.

## What didn't
- **Actions minutes.**
  - About 30 ten-minute CI runs in one day hit the free allowance, and CI stopped (2026-11-10, 14:15 UTC). Adam raised the limit.
  - Stacked PRs made it worse: every stacked PR runs a full job.
  - **Changes:**
    - small fixes and features are bundled into one PR, pushed rarely, with no stacks;
    - pushes to `dev` don't re-run the suite;
    - docs-only PRs skip the build and Playwright (ADR 0015, amended).
- **Retargeting a stacked PR didn't start CI.** Change the base first, then push.
- **Cancelled at the time limit.** With four runs at once, one job hit its 15-minute limit after its tests had passed. A cancelled run isn't counted as green.
- **Learning results:** still none measured. Adam hasn't yet played M2 or M3 for long, and the Definition of Done's "Adam tried it" is open on #38 and #39 (E8, M2).

## Next time
- Plan the CI cost of a milestone up front: one PR per feature, about one run each.
- Run the full Playwright suite locally once before opening a PR, so CI runs once.
- Ask Adam to play one Morse and one Arabic flow a week, and note what he learned. Without that, the retros can't say whether Waymark teaches.
